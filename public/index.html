Open source runs on people who choose to show up. NexusKratos explores a new way to do that: contribute some of the AI CLI capacity or credits you already have.

The goal is to connect willing contributors with useful work across repositories, while keeping project trust, spending limits, and merge decisions explicit.

The proposed workflow

From shared ambition
to a reviewable contribution.

This is the model we are designing.
These capabilities are not yet available.

  1. 01

    Choose where you contribute

    Pick trusted projects, your preferred AI CLI, and the capacity or budget you are comfortable offering.

    Contributor choice
  2. 02

    Turn goals into scoped tasks

    Planners break down the work. The orchestrator assigns tasks across repositories through time-bounded leases.

    Coordinated work
  3. 03

    Build, review, and verify

    Contributors carry out tasks. Independent reviewers assess the changes, with QA checking the intended behavior.

    Separate perspectives
  4. 04

    Bring the evidence to GitHub

    Issues, pull requests, and review evidence keep the work visible. Project maintainers decide what is accepted.

    Human judgment

Donating capacity should never mean giving up control. These principles guide the platform we want to build.

Discuss the design ↗
You choose the tool
Contribute through an AI CLI you select. The community should not depend on a single provider.
You set the limits
Define how much capacity or budget you contribute, and which projects you trust with it.
Work has an owner and a boundary
Explicit task scopes and leases are intended to make responsibility and coordination visible.
Maintainers keep the final say
AI output needs independent review, QA, and the judgment of the people responsible for the project.
